PALEONTOLOGY

A lifelong passion.

My passion for ancient life started before I could walk. Below are the areas that have kept me curious.

01

Vertebrate Paleontology

Understanding extinct vertebrates, their anatomy, evolution, and relationships across deep time.

02

Paleobiology

Using fossils to ask how extinct organisms lived, functioned, adapted, and evolved.

03

Paleoecology

Reconstructing ancient communities and environmental change through interactions among organisms and their surroundings.

04

Taphonomy

Understanding how organisms become fossilized and how preservation shapes the evidence available to researchers.

05

Evolutionary Paleontology

Following major evolutionary transitions and the fossil evidence behind them.

06

Geobiology

Exploring the relationships between Earth's changing environments and the evolution of life.

CURRENT PHILOSOPHY

Experience first. Specialize later.

My current goal is to gain enough geological, biological, field, and laboratory experience to make an informed decision about a future research specialty rather than choosing one too early.
